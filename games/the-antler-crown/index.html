The Antler Crown

Wintersedge has not seen a true spring in three years.
The Antler King's crown was found broken at the tree line, and winter never broke with it. Old Mrend can no longer walk the Wyld herself — so she is handing her oath to you. What you swear at the threshold stone, the Wyld will hold you to. Restore the old Covenant, forge a new one, or end it for good. The choice is yours, chapter by chapter, and the ending remembers everything you chose to become along the way.
A single sitting runs 45–70 minutes. Four endings, each earned differently.
